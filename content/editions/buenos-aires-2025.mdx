Invisible Garden's second gathering ran for three weeks in Buenos Aires, timed
around Devconnect. 1,038 developers applied and 45 builders came on site. Over
40 mentors ran 69 workshops across the three weeks, and the cohort produced 27
open-source projects and research outputs.

The closing ceremony brought together speakers from across the Ethereum
ecosystem. Sponsors were Ethereum Everywhere, Arbitrum DAO, AltLayer and ZKM.
