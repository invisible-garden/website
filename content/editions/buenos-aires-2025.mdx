Invisible Garden's second gathering ran for three weeks in Buenos Aires, timed
around Devconnect. 1,038 developers applied, 379 were accepted, and 45 builders
came on site.

Over 40 mentors ran 69 workshops across the three weeks. The cohort produced 27
open-source projects and research outputs, and the closing ceremony brought
together speakers from across the Ethereum ecosystem.

Ethereum Everywhere, Arbitrum DAO, AltLayer and ZKM sponsored the edition, and
the LATAM Ethereum communities carried much of the local weight.
